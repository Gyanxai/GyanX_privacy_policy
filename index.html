We do not sell personal data. We do not use children's personal data for advertising, tracking or behavioural profiling, provided this statement accurately reflects the Services, integrations and third-party SDKs actually used.

6. How we share information

We do not sell personal data. When a parent account is linked to a student account by scanning the student's unique QR code, GyanX makes the student's feature-usage time and subject-level learning progress available in the Parent App. We may also share information:

  • with the service providers identified in Section 5, to the extent needed to operate the Services;
  • with a parent or legal guardian through the Parent App, only to the extent permitted by the actual account-linking, consent and access-control features;
  • when required by applicable law, court order or valid legal process;
  • when reasonably necessary to protect the rights, safety or security of users, GyanX or the public, in accordance with applicable law; or

We do not permit service providers to use personal data for their own unrelated purposes where prohibited by applicable law or our applicable agreements. The actual data shared depends on the feature and provider.

7. Parent and child accounts

The Student App and Parent App may provide different features and access to different categories of information. Where parent-child account linking is available, GyanX uses the relationship to provide the parental features enabled for the linked account.

Account-linking method: A student creates an account in the Student App and receives a unique QR code associated with that account. A parent or legal guardian can scan the student's QR code in the Parent App to link the parent account to the student's account. The QR code is used to establish the account link; it should not be treated as proof of legal guardianship unless GyanX separately verifies that relationship through an appropriate process.

Information visible to parents: Once accounts are linked, the Parent App displays the student's learning progress, including the time spent using different features and progress in each subject. The Parent App's access is intended for these progress and usage summaries; this Policy does not state that parents can view AI chat transcripts, uploaded homework images or voice recordings unless those features are actually available to them.

Parents should keep the student's QR code private and share it only with the intended parent or legal guardian. GyanX should provide a way to review and remove an account link, and should apply appropriate safeguards to prevent unauthorised linking. Parents or legal guardians may contact us to ask about, correct or request deletion of a linked child's information, subject to applicable law and appropriate verification.
