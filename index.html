GyanX Privacy Policy

Effective date: 13 October 2026 · Last updated: 13 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Gyanx Private Limited (“GyanX”, “we”, “us” or “our”) collects, uses, shares, stores and protects personal data when you use the GyanX student application (“Student App”), the GyanX parent application (“Parent App”), the website at gyanx.in, and related services (together, the “Services”).

The Student App includes educational features such as:

  • OneScan: scanning or photographing a question to receive an explanation or answer.
  • Talk to Teacher: chatting with or speaking to an AI tutor about school subjects and curriculum.

The features available may differ by app, account type, region or version. This Policy should be read together with notices shown when you create an account, give consent or enable a feature.

1. Who this Policy is for

The Services are intended for students and their parents or legal guardians. Students may be under 18, so the Student App and Parent App may process information about both children and adults.

Where required by applicable law, a parent or legal guardian must provide consent before a child uses the Services. GyanX may request reasonable information to verify the adult's identity and authority. A parent or guardian may contact us to review, correct or request deletion of a child's personal data, or to withdraw consent.

We do not sell or rent personal data. We do not use children's personal data for targeted advertising or behavioural profiling.

3. App permissions

  • Camera and photo library: used when you choose to scan or upload a question.
  • Microphone: used for voice input in supported features.
  • Notifications: used for reminders and service updates if you grant permission.
  • Internet access: required for sign-in, server communication and online features.

You can manage available permissions through your device settings. The apps request camera, photo-library or microphone access when it is needed for a feature selected by you. If you deny or withdraw a permission, the related feature may not work, but unrelated features remain available.

4. How we use information

Depending on the app and feature, we use information to:

  • create, authenticate, maintain and secure accounts;
  • provide scanning, AI tutoring, lessons, quizzes and explanations;
  • personalise content based on class, language, subjects and learning progress;
  • provide parent features and display student information that the parent is authorised to access;
  • send service messages and notifications where enabled;
  • detect abuse, investigate security issues, troubleshoot errors and improve reliability and safety;
  • respond to support requests, handle privacy requests and comply with applicable legal obligations;
  • respond to website enquiries from students, parents, teachers and prospective partners; and
  • measure website usage and campaign performance when the visitor has provided the relevant cookie consent.

We will not use personal data for a materially different purpose without providing any notice or obtaining any consent required by applicable law.

6. How we share information

We do not sell or rent personal data. When a parent account is linked to a student account by scanning the student's unique QR code, GyanX makes the student's feature-usage time and subject-level learning progress available in the Parent App. We may also share information:

  • with the service providers identified in Section 5, to the extent needed to operate the Services;
  • with a parent or legal guardian through the Parent App, only to the extent permitted by the actual account-linking, consent and access-control features;
  • when required by applicable law, court order or valid legal process;
  • when reasonably necessary to protect the rights, safety or security of users, GyanX or the public, in accordance with applicable law; or
  • in connection with a merger, restructuring, acquisition or transfer of the Services, subject to applicable law and appropriate safeguards.

We do not permit service providers to use personal data for their own unrelated purposes where prohibited by applicable law or our applicable agreements. The actual data shared depends on the feature and provider.

7. Parent and child accounts

The Student App and Parent App may provide different features and access to different categories of information. Where parent-child account linking is available, GyanX uses the relationship to provide the parental features enabled for the linked account.

Account-linking method: A student creates an account in the Student App and receives a unique QR code associated with that account. A parent or legal guardian can scan the student's QR code in the Parent App to link the parent account to the student's account. The QR code is used to establish the account link; it should not be treated as proof of legal guardianship unless GyanX separately verifies that relationship through an appropriate process.

Information visible to parents: Once accounts are linked, the Parent App displays the student's learning progress, including the time spent using different features and progress in each subject. The Parent App's access is intended for these progress and usage summaries; this Policy does not state that parents can view AI chat transcripts, uploaded homework images or voice recordings unless those features are actually available to them.

Parents should keep the student's QR code private and share it only with the intended parent or legal guardian. Scanning the QR code establishes a technical account link; it is not, by itself, proof of legal guardianship. Parents or legal guardians may contact us to review or remove a link or to ask about, correct or request deletion of a linked child's information, subject to appropriate verification.

8. Data storage, international transfers and security

Personal data may be stored or processed on infrastructure operated by GyanX and its service providers. The relevant locations depend on the services and configurations used. Data may be processed in India or in other countries where a provider or its subprocessors operate, subject to applicable legal requirements.

We use safeguards designed to protect personal data, including authentication, access controls and restricted administrative access. No method of transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Data retention and deletion

GyanX retains personal data only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Services, fulfil the purposes described in this Policy, maintain security and meet applicable legal obligations. Retention periods may differ by category and provider.

Account and profile information, parent-child links, learning progress and related service records are retained while the account is active and afterwards only where needed for legal compliance, security, fraud prevention or dispute resolution. Website enquiry information is retained until the enquiry and reasonable follow-up are complete. Locally saved lecture recordings remain on the device until the user removes them. Technical logs, backups and provider-held copies may be retained for their documented operational cycles before deletion or anonymisation.

Requesting account and data deletion

You can request deletion by emailing support@gyanx.in with the subject line Delete my GyanX account. Include the phone number, email address or account identifier needed to locate the account. Do not send a password, OTP or unnecessary sensitive information.

Parents or legal guardians may request deletion of a child's account or linked information, subject to appropriate verification and applicable law. We may ask for reasonable information to verify the request and protect the account from unauthorised deletion.

After verifying a request, GyanX will process it within the timeframe required by applicable law. We will delete or anonymise data in our systems and request deletion from relevant service providers where applicable, subject to legal retention obligations and technical limitations that are explained to the requester. Some records may need to be retained for a legally required period or for a legitimate security or dispute-resolution purpose.

Chat history may be stored locally on your device and, depending on the feature, on GyanX servers or provider systems. Uninstalling the app or clearing its local data does not necessarily delete information held on servers or by providers.

This section is GyanX's public account-deletion request resource. It can be accessed directly at https://gyanxai.github.io/GyanX_privacy_policy/#account-deletion.
